A01   The Three Highest Immortals      14x9x3 inch






Fu 福   Lu 禄   Shou 寿  

The Three Stars:
Regarded as manifestations of the Tao and origin of all sentient beings.
The three highest Tao immortals Xiān(仙), embodying the Taoist ideals of wisdom, spiritual transformation, and longevity.

Shou 寿 'Star of Longevity' (figure on the right):
recognized by his high, domed forehead,
in one hand a crooked staff, calabash double gourd with elixir of life,
in the other hand a peach as symbol of immortality.

Lu 禄 'Star of Prosperity' (figure between other two):
is holding a ruyi (sceptre).

Fu 福 'Star of Fortune' (figure on the left):
depicted in scholar dress, holding a scroll
