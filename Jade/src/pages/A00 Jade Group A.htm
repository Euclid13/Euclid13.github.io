A01   The Three Highest Immortals     14x9x3 inch


The Three Highest Immortals

     Fu ( 福 )    Shou ( 寿 )    Lu ( 禄 )   


The Three Stars:
Regarded as manifestations of the Tao and origin of all sentient beings.
The three highest Tao immortals Xiān(仙), embodying the Taoist ideals of wisdom, spiritual transformation, and longevity.

Fu ( 福 )    'Star of Fortune'   (figure on the left):
depicted in scholar dress, holding a scroll

Shou ( 寿 )    'Star of Longevity'   (figure on the right):
recognized by his high, domed forehead,
in one hand a crooked staff, calabash double gourd with elixir of life,
in the other hand a peach as symbol of immortality.

Lu ( 禄 )    'Star of Prosperity'   (figure between other two):
is holding a ruyi (sceptre).

GYI

A10   Guan Yin, Immortals      27x19 inch


A skillfully rendered relief carving.

The folds of the fabric draped over her appears to be of fine, light weight fabric, flowing down to her feet.

There is a vessel at her feet, from which is emanating elixir of spiritual transformation.

She is holding a lotus stalk in various stages of life;
a flower beginning to open, a flower fully open, a flower withered (leaving seed pod for rebirth).

At her breast is lingzhi mushroom, symbolizing good fortune and longevity, the carving of which incorporated a small apple-green inclusion.
