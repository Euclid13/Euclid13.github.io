Jade Group B



B04   Hotai      24x20 inch


Hotai is often depicted as a cheerful, rotund monk, carrying a cloth sack, said to contain endless treasures.  This imagery conveys a message of generosity, the joy in giving, and the idea that true happiness and prosperity come from spiritual rather than material wealth.
