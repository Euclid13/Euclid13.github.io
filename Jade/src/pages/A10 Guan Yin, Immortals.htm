A10   Guan Yin, Immortals     27x19 inch


A skillfully rendered relief carving.

The folds of the fabric draped over her appears to be of fine, light weight fabric, flowing down to her feet.

There is a vessel at her feet, from which is emanating elixir of spiritual transformation.

She is holding a lotus stalk in various stages of life;
a flower beginning to open, a flower fully open, a flower withered (leaving seed pod for rebirth).

At her breast is lingzhi mushroom, symbolizing good fortune and longevity, the carving of which incorporated a small apple-green inclusion.
