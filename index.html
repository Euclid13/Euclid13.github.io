Welcome to our gallery.
We have an extensive collection of Chinese hand carved jade art.  
All of the pieces draw inspiration from the spirituality of Taoism, Buddhism, and other subjects of traditional Chinese culture.

We are currently in the process of identifying and working with people who are qualified to do valuations of jade carvings.   When you find a piece that is of particular interest, these are the same people who we can commission for a Written Appraisal.   This is a formal document which states the name and credentials of appraiser; an accurate description of the piece, numerous photos, and involves further effort to research more comparisons for a more accurate valuation.   This will be available for your review prior to purchase and included as part of shipping.
