Welcome to our gallery.
We have an extensive collection of Chinese hand carved jade art.  All of the pieces draw inspiration from the spirituality of Taoism, Buddhism, and other subjects of traditional Chinese culture.

The indicated prices have been determined by the assessments of people with decades of experience doing appraisals of Chinese art, including jade carvings.  These prices are an estimate, by people that are recognized for their integrity and impartial valuations. 

If you have an interest in a particular piece, we can commission a Written Appraisal.  This is a formal document which states the name and credentials of appraiser; an accurate description of the piece, numerous photos, and involves further effort to research more comparisons for a more accurate valuation.  This would be available for your review prior to purchase and included as part of shipping.
